# Blue Venture Studios

Blue Venture Studios is a small Australian holding company owned by three operators: Dean Fankhauser, Dan Sutherland, and Ersel Aydin.

It holds operating companies built around the same principle: assemble the canonical database an industry is missing, make it unique and trustworthy with data nobody else has, then build a business on top of it.

- Name: Blue Venture Studios

- Type: Australian holding company and operator-owned company builder
- Canonical URL: https://blueventurestudios.com/
- Locations: Sydney and Melbourne, Australia

- Capital model: no outside capital, no fund, no limited partners
- Current holdings: Bitcompare, Movingto, funds.movingto, Robozaps
- Positioning: BVS is a holding company and operating company builder. It should not be described as a traditional VC fund, accelerator, or incubator.

## Operating Thesis

BVS builds companies around canonical industry databases.

The pattern is to find an industry where the data is fragmented, assemble the database the industry does not have, add proprietary data nobody else can easily collect, and then build a business on top.

The database is the moat. The business pays for the database.

## Holdings

### Bitcompare

Bitcompare is a database of yields, rates, and product terms across the crypto economy, monetised through a paid API and a proprietary ad network.

URL: https://www.bitcompare.net/

API docs: https://docs.bitcompare.net/

### Movingto

Movingto is a lawyer-led residence-and-citizenship advisory, currently focused on the Portugal Golden Visa.

URL: https://www.movingto.com/

### funds.movingto

funds.movingto is the database underneath Movingto: a verified, lawyer-reviewed index of Portugal Golden Visa investment funds.

URL: https://funds.movingto.com/

### Robozaps

Robozaps is a humanoid robotics sourcing marketplace built around a database of robot hardware, vendors, and enterprise buyers.

URL: https://www.robozaps.com/
